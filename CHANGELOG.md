# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

* A Markers panel in the map's Filters menu, one button per type, in six groups: Harvesting (trees, ores, digging and
  fishing spots), Cooking (fish, herbs, food), Wild Animals, Enemies (the "Kill N" challenge factions), Valuables
  (treasure chests, special shinies, keys not picked up yet) and Navigation (whispers not activated yet, shortcuts not
  opened yet, Dire Nettles). Each group can be switched off in Options > Gameplay; Cooking, Valuables and Navigation
  start off.
* Markers follow the world's state: areas change their enemies with story progress and over time. A plain marker is
  there now; a marker with a "?" can be there when you arrive. The "?" button next to the panel's title hides the maybes.
* Harvested, killed and looted spots get a red cross until the game brings them back (when the fog creeps back), right
  after the action, also on the Minimap mod's minimap.
* What is inside caves and cellars shows at their entrance; waiting at a cave's entrance shows the moment it rolls the
  enemy you are after.
* The game's height arrows on markers near you; marker size and markers in unexplored areas in Options > Gameplay.
* Buttons appear once the story allows the type in your world and turn grey while none is around; names in the game's
  language.
* Gamepad (D-pad and left stick) and keyboard navigation like the game's own filters.
